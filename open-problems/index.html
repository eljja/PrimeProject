What TICKET-265 actually changed

TICKET-265 did not solve a parent conjecture. It refutes density-one replacement of the RH all-index envelope, separates unbounded Collatz cutoffs from divergence, proves the sharp growing two-adic Goldbach tie threshold, and adds a necessary but insufficient mod-32 Twin filter.

TICKET-265는 상위 난제를 해결하지 않았습니다. RH density-one 대체와 Collatz 비유계 cutoff 충분성을 폐기하고, Goldbach 성장형 2-adic tie 임계치와 필요한 mod-32 Twin 필터를 확정했습니다.
