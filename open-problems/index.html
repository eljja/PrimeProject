What TICKET-185 actually changed

TICKET-185 did not solve a conjecture. It proves spectral escape under two neutral moments, excludes an infinite Collatz cycle stratum, identifies each finite Goldbach target's exact factor horizon, and proves the integer-granularity obstruction to symmetric Twin block certificates.

TICKET-185는 난제를 해결하지 않았습니다. 리만에서는 두 중립 모멘트만으로 꼬리 긴밀성을 얻는 경로를 반박했고, 콜라츠에서는 한 번의 v=1과 나머지 v=2인 무한 주기족을 배제했습니다. 골드바흐에서는 목표별 정확 인수 지평을, 쌍둥이 소수에서는 작은 주항에서 대칭 절댓값 인증이 불가능한 정수 격자 경계를 확립했습니다. 다음 산술 보조정리의 무한 양화사는 모두 미증명입니다.

Riemann Hypothesis 리만 가설

Exact result / 확립
Two neutral moments still admit positive-definite autocorrelations whose spectral mass escapes every fixed band.
No-go / 폐기
Support, positivity, normalization, and two neutral moments do not force spectral-tail tightness.

Weil-form coercivity modulo spectral translations on an explicit pole-neutral core.

02 · GLOBAL DESCENTOPEN

Collatz Conjecture 콜라츠 추측

Exact result / 확립
Every primitive cycle word with exactly one valuation one and all other valuations two fails affine divisibility.
No-go / 폐기

03 · SIGNED MINOR ARCSOPEN

Strong Goldbach 강한 골드바흐 추측

Exact result / 확립
The target-specific factor horizon is the exact cutoff that removes every bad candidate pair.
No-go / 폐기

Signed cancellation of bad survivors below the exact horizon stays under the target margin.

04 · PRIME-PAIR OPERATORSOPEN

Twin Prime 쌍둥이 소수 추측

Exact result / 확립
The one-sided remainder condition is exactly positivity; symmetric domination is impossible when expected mass is at most one half.

Absolute remainder domination is stronger than the integer-valued existence question.
