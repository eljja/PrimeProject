What TICKET-263 actually changed

TICKET-263 did not solve a parent conjecture. It proves the optimal factor-two reciprocal envelope for RH-style packet lags, diagonalizes all fixed Weyl limits into one growing cutoff, refines the q=3 Goldbach tie obstruction to four phases modulo 32, and makes the Twin joint ninth-order congruence pair exact beyond an explicit root-cone threshold.

TICKET-263은 상위 난제를 해결하지 않았습니다. RH reciprocal envelope의 최적 상수 2, Collatz growing-cutoff 대각화, Goldbach mod-32 네 위상, Twin root cone의 9차 합동 필요충분 판정을 확정했습니다.
